01 / About

A researcher's path, taken early

I'm a computer engineering student in the Barnard University remote scholar program, and I'm genuinely into STEM, especially AI, ML, mathematics, and quantum mechanics. My path into quantum started sideways: a curiosity about the philosophy and theory of mathematics pulled me deeper and deeper until quantum mechanics sparked something I haven't stopped chasing since.

Right now I'm reading Nielsen & Chuang's Quantum Computation and Quantum Information alongside Stewart's PreCalculus. Not because either is easy, but because the surest way I know to grow is to sit with material that's slightly out of reach. People around me have said these books are very advanced. I think that's kind of the point.
